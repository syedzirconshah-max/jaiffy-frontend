❓ Help & Guide
Everything you need to know about using Jaiffy
🚀 Getting Started ▼
How do I create an account?
Click "Create Account" on the login page. Enter your name, email and password. You will receive a 6-digit verification code via email — enter it to activate your account. You get a 7-day free trial with 30 leads/day automatically.
I didn't receive the verification email. What should I do?
Check your Spam or Junk folder first — this is the most common reason. If not there, click "Resend" on the verification screen. Make sure you used a valid Gmail, Yahoo or Outlook email.
The verify banner keeps appearing every time I log in.
This means your email is not yet verified. Enter the 6-digit code from your email in the banner at the top of the dashboard. Once verified, the banner will disappear permanently.
Is Jaiffy free to use?
Yes — you get a 7-day free trial with 30 leads per day. After the trial, the free plan gives you 10 leads per day. For unlimited searches and full contact details, upgrade to Pro (Rs.3,000/month).
🔍 Searching for Leads ▼
How do I search for buyers?
Go to Lead Search from the sidebar. Type your product (e.g. "surgical instruments", "leather gloves", "textile fabric"). Select the target country and click Search. AI will find real businesses and score them automatically.
What should I type in the Product field?
Type what you export or sell. Examples: "surgical instruments", "sports goods importers", "leather goods wholesalers", "rice exporters". Be specific for better results. You can also try category names like "medical equipment buyers".
What is the City/State field for?
This is optional. Leave it blank to search the entire country. Type a city name (e.g. "Dubai", "London", "New York") to narrow results to that specific area. This gives more targeted leads.
Why is the search taking a long time?
Jaiffy uses AI to analyse every business — this takes 30-90 seconds for accurate scoring. A progress bar shows what is happening. Please wait — do not refresh the page during search.
Why do some leads show "Phone not found" or "No email"?
Not all businesses list their contact information publicly on Google Maps. If a phone or email is missing, it means Google Maps does not have it. You can visit their website directly or search them on Google for more information.
What does the AI Score (0-100) mean?
The AI scores each business based on: availability of phone (+20), website (+15), email (+20), Google rating (+10), and number of reviews (+5). A score above 70 means a High quality lead worth contacting first.
📞 Contacts & Data ▼
Why are phone numbers and emails blurred/locked?
Full contact details (phone, email, website) are available on the Pro plan (Rs.3,000/month). On the free plan, you can see the business name, address, rating and AI score — but contacts are hidden. Upgrade to Pro to unlock everything.
I am on Pro but contacts are still not showing.
Make sure your Pro plan is active — check Settings to see your current plan. If it shows "Free", contact admin on WhatsApp. Also note that some businesses simply do not have public contact info on Google Maps — this is not a system error.
The phone number shown doesn't match the business website.
Jaiffy fetches phone numbers from Google Maps — businesses sometimes register different numbers on Google vs their website. Both numbers may be valid. Always double-check by visiting their website.
✉️ AI Email Generator ▼
How do I send an email to a lead?
Click the Email button on any lead card. Choose "AI Generate" for an automatic professional email, or "Write Yourself" to use your own template. Copy the generated email and paste it into your Gmail or Outlook to send.
Can Jaiffy send emails directly?
No — Jaiffy generates the email content for you. You copy it and send from your own email account (Gmail, Outlook, etc.). This keeps your emails authentic and avoids spam filters.
How do I add my company website and portfolio to emails?
Go to Settings and fill in: Company Website, Portfolio/Catalog Link, and Company Description. Once saved, this information will be automatically included in every AI-generated email.
"AI temporarily unavailable" — what does this mean?
The AI (Gemini) service is temporarily at capacity. This usually resolves in a few minutes. You can use the "Write Yourself" tab to write your own email in the meantime.

What is included in the Pro plan?
Pro plan (Rs.3,000/month) includes: 100 leads per day, full contact details (phone, email, website), AI email generator, CSV export, lead history, and data refresh. Best for active exporters.
Is there a refund policy?
Contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of payment if you have any issue. We resolve all concerns fairly — your satisfaction matters to us.

👥 Referral & Wallet ▼
How does the referral program work?
Share your referral link with friends. When they join on the free plan, you get +10 leads/day bonus (up to 5 friends = +50 leads/day). When a friend upgrades to Pro, you earn Rs.500 cashback in your wallet.
How do I withdraw my wallet balance?
Go to Referral page. When your balance reaches Rs.1,000 minimum, the "Withdraw" button activates. Enter your JazzCash/EasyPaisa number and submit. Admin will transfer within 24 hours.
Can I get 1 month Pro free through referrals?
Yes! Refer 20 friends who sign up using your link — you get 1 month Pro absolutely free. Track your progress on the Referral page.
🔧 Technical Issues ▼
"Server Connection Failed" — what do I do?
1. Check your internet connection (WiFi or mobile data). 2. Wait 30 seconds and try again. 3. Refresh the page. 4. If the problem continues, contact us on WhatsApp — we will fix it quickly.
Search is not returning any results.
Try a different keyword — be more specific or more general. Make sure the country is correct. Some niche products may have fewer results in certain countries. Also check if you have reached your daily lead limit.
The website works better on desktop — why?
Jaiffy is designed primarily for desktop/laptop use because the lead search table and email generator work best on larger screens. Mobile support is available but some features may appear compact. We recommend using a laptop for the best experience.
My daily limit reset — when does it happen?
Daily limits reset at midnight Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) every day. If you have used all your leads for the day, come back after midnight or refer friends to get bonus leads.
